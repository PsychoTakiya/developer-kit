# 8-Week Interview Study Plan

Built for a full-stack JavaScript engineer with about 3.5 years of experience targeting **SWE II / Senior** roles, studying around **10–12 hours a week** alongside a full-time job. Compress it to 4–5 weeks if you have interviews soon. Do weeks 1–2 regardless, because they unlock everything else.

Tick tasks off as you go: checkboxes are saved in this browser, and [My Progress](./progress) shows how far through each week you are.

## What each interview round needs

| Round | Kit section | Weight for SWE II / Senior |
|---|---|---|
| Coding (DSA) | [DSA Patterns](/dsa/01-patterns), [Problem Tracker](/dsa/03-problem-tracker) | High at product companies, medium elsewhere |
| Machine coding / JS round | [Implement It Yourself](/javascript/03-implement-yourself), [JS Core](/javascript/01-core-language) | High for frontend/full-stack |
| System design | [Framework](/system-design/00-interview-framework), [Case Studies](/system-design/06-case-studies) | High; this is where the Senior level is decided |
| Frontend system design | [Frontend System Design](/system-design/07-frontend-system-design) | High for frontend-leaning roles |
| Tech depth | React, Node, [Databases](/databases/01-sql-fundamentals), [Web Fundamentals](/web-fundamentals/01-http-networking) | Medium–high |
| Behavioral / hiring manager | [Story Bank](/behavioral/01-story-bank), [Project Deep Dive](/behavioral/02-intro-and-project-deep-dive) | High; decides level |

## Daily rhythm (about 1.5–2 hours on weekdays)

1. **15 min, flashcards:** a [review session](./review) of the cards due today, answered out loud. The kit schedules each card's next review from your rating.
2. **45–60 min, DSA:** one new problem from the tracker plus any redos due today.
3. **30–45 min, the week's topic:** read, then close the page and explain it out loud or on paper.

**Weekends (3–4 hours):** one mock interview, one system design case end to end, story practice, applications.

## The day before an interview

1. Read the relevant night-before sheet: [React](/react/16-interview-cheatsheet), [Node](/node-express/12-node-express-cheatsheet), or [System Design flashcards](/system-design/flashcards).
2. Say your intro pitch and two STAR stories out loud.
3. Solve one easy problem to warm up. Don't start anything new.
4. Prepare your setup: quiet room, charged laptop, editor, notepad, water.
5. Sleep. A rested brain recalls far better than a crammed one.

## Why this method works

- **Active recall:** testing yourself (flashcards, solving from scratch, explaining out loud) builds memory far better than re-reading.
- **Spaced repetition:** reviewing just before you'd forget (3, 7, 21 days) makes knowledge last.
- **Interleaving:** mixing DSA, design and tech depth each week beats studying one subject for a month.
- **Speaking out loud:** interviews are spoken. Practising silently hides gaps that show up when you talk.
