# Flashcard Review

Practise every flashcard deck in one place, one card at a time. The kit schedules each card using **spaced repetition**: cards you miss come back tomorrow, and cards you know come back after 3, 7, 14 days and longer.

**How it works:** read the question, answer **out loud**, reveal the answer, then rate yourself honestly. Ten to twenty cards a day beats a long cram the night before.
